# Nibras Feature Descriptions

This document describes the two Nibras features to introduce in the promo video: AI tutor chat and AI explanations for supported exam questions.

## 1. Nibras AI Tutor

Nibras is Learnmates’ conversational AI tutor for supported Biology, Chemistry, and Physics subjects across IGCSE, Cambridge/CAIE A-Level, and Edexcel International A-Level (IAL). A student chooses a subject workspace, asks a question, reads the response, and can continue with follow-up questions.

Nibras is designed to help students explore concepts and prepare for exams. Its responses are developed with past papers, mark schemes, and syllabus or specification content in mind. It is an AI tool and can make mistakes, so students should check anything uncertain.

**What to show in the video**

1. Open Nibras from Learnmates.
2. Show the selected subject and level.
3. Enter a short subject question and send it.
4. Pause on a useful section of the answer, then show a follow-up if it fits the video.

**Sample prompt for a demo:**  
“Can you explain how current changes in a series circuit?”

## 2. AI Explanations for Exam Questions

On supported topical questions and individual past-paper questions where the feature is available, students can select **Explain** to ask Nibras for help understanding the question and how to work toward an answer. For multiple-choice questions, the explanation can cover the options; for written questions, students can ask about individual parts or check their answers part by part. After reading the explanation, they can choose **Ask follow-up** to clarify a step, idea, or method.

This feature is available for supported Biology, Chemistry, and Physics question content. The question and, when needed, its mark scheme provide context for the explanation. Availability can depend on the question and the materials provided.

**What to show in the video**

1. Open a supported topical question that has its required question content and answer or mark scheme.
2. Keep the question visible, then click **Explain**.
3. Show the resulting explanation and its structure.
4. Click **Ask follow-up**, enter a short clarification question, and show the answer.

**Sample follow-up:**  
“Can you explain why that step is needed?”

## Important Note for Viewers

Nibras is an AI tutor, not a guarantee of correct answers. Its responses can be wrong or incomplete. Encourage students to double-check important information and report anything that seems incorrect.
